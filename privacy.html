This Privacy Policy explains how Diobity (the “Bot”), a Discord assistant developed by Fouad (the “Developer”), processes information when you interact with it. The Bot provides AI conversations, optional web searches, and information about the Miracul212 Minecraft server and modpack.

1. Information processed

  • Discord user, server, and channel identifiers needed to deliver responses and apply settings.
  • Message content sent directly to the Bot, including direct messages, mentions, replies to the Bot, and messages invoking the Bot by name.
  • Conversation context saved per channel in local JSON files, subject to the Bot's configured memory behavior.
  • Server-specific configuration, such as allowed channels, response language, cooldowns, and Minecraft information.
  • Technical information needed to diagnose failures, which may appear in application logs.

2. How information is used

Information is used to generate replies, preserve conversation context, apply server preferences, provide Minecraft server status, prevent abuse, and maintain the Bot. The Bot does not need to read unrelated messages for conversation history, but Discord message events may be processed to determine whether the Bot was addressed.

3. Third-party services

When an AI response is generated, relevant message content and conversation context may be sent to Groq for processing. When you explicitly request a web search, the search query may be sent to Tavily; search results may also be included in the context provided to Groq. Discord processes information according to its own policies. Third-party providers may process data under their respective terms and privacy policies.

4. Storage and retention

Conversation context and server settings may be stored as JSON files on the Bot's hosting system. Data remains stored until it is removed by the operator, through any available deletion controls, or as part of maintenance. No fixed automatic deletion period is promised. Backups and logs, if enabled by the operator, may have separate retention periods.

5. Sharing

Information is shared with service providers only as needed to operate requested features, as described above, or when required by applicable law. The Developer does not claim to sell personal information.

6. Security

Reasonable technical measures, such as storing API credentials outside public source code and restricting administrative settings, should be used to protect information. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and deletion requests

You may stop using the Bot at any time. Server administrators may adjust available settings. To request access to or deletion of stored conversation information, contact the Developer using the contact method listed below, and provide enough detail to identify the relevant Discord server or channel. Requests are handled subject to reasonable verification and applicable obligations.

8. Children

Use of the Bot is subject to Discord's minimum age requirements and applicable laws. Do not provide sensitive personal information in conversations with the Bot.

9. Changes

This Policy may be updated as features, providers, or data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when material updates are published.
